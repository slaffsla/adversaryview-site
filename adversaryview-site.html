The Adversary View

An independent perspective. Practical decisions.

Ingressline Secure is built around a simple idea: understand the weaknesses before deciding what to buy or change. The goal is not to sell you another camera or alarm. It is to help you make informed security decisions based on the property, its surroundings and the way it is actually used.

  • Entry points, doors, windows and perimeter access
  • Visibility, lighting, blind spots and camera coverage
  • Access routines, deliveries and everyday operational context
  • How existing security measures work together
  • Prioritised recommendations with practical next steps
Human-led, on-site assessment

Some things are best understood in person.

For properties in Israel, an on-site physical security assessment adds a real-world review of the property and its surroundings. The visit is followed by a written report outlining findings, priority areas and practical recommendations.

The scope can include entry points, perimeter access, visibility, lighting, camera coverage, access routines and relevant operational factors.
